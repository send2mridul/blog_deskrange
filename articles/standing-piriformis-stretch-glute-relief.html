The Movement Science Behind Standing Piriformis Stretches

Why Long Intense Workouts Cannot Undo All-Day Sitting

Many desk workers assume that an hour of intense gym training in the evening cancels out eight hours of unbroken seated immobility. However, orthopedic research shows that prolonged static sitting causes physiological stiffness that cannot be fully reversed by a single workout.

Spine health requires frequent, gentle movement snacks distributed throughout your workday rather than one punishing evening workout.
